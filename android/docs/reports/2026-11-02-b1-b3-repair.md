# B1–B3 mobile repairs — 2 November 2026

All three confirmed findings from the [follow-up audit](2026-11-01-new-mobile-bug-audit.md) are repaired in source. Baseline: `6076a2906a45346e2a47575b5fc7738e933cd630`. Branch: `fix/b1-b3-mobile-regressions`. Test evidence was collected in the 1 November repair session; this report was finalized on 2 November.

## Changes

| Finding | Changed source | Result |
|---|---|---|
| B1: stale failed records cannot be removed | `lib/providers/download_provider.dart` | Close/Cancel removes a failed local task when native reports that its ID no longer belongs to this app. Other errors and live tasks retain their records. Native ownership guards remain unchanged. Removing stale records frees queue capacity. |
| B2: direct audio-only MP4/WebM treated as video | `lib/services/video_parser_service.dart` | Direct-file inspection uses the reported audio MIME, including mixed case and parameters. Audio kind survives queue persistence and drives Open/Share MIME selection. Missing MIME retains extension-based behavior. |
| B3: HTTP 103 treated as final media response | `android/app/src/main/kotlin/com/turbosave/videodownloader/PublicMediaProbe.kt` | Preflight reads past informational responses to the final status and headers. It accepts at most 16 informational blocks, retains the shared header/time limits, and rejects protocol switching as unsupported. Redirect, address, TLS and media checks remain in place. |

## Regression evidence

Added 13 Dart cases in `test/new_audit_regression_test.dart` and `test/cleanup_and_direct_audio_test.dart`, and 10 JVM cases in `InformationalResponseTest.kt`. No existing tests were edited, deleted or weakened. No dependencies were added.

Before the production changes, the new Dart regressions produced **6 passes and 7 failures**. The initial native regressions produced **1 pass and 8 failures**. Those failures reproduced the reported behaviors. After the fixes, all 13 focused Dart checks and the initial 9 native checks passed; an additional 16-block boundary case is included in the final native suite.

The new tests cover stale-record removal and queue exhaustion; retention after unrelated cancellation errors; direct audio Open/Share after restoration; missing-MIME fallback; and HTTP informational response chains, final redirects, chunked media, protocol switching and resource bounds. The four original audit reproductions were copied unchanged into the maintained regression suite.
